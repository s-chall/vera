Inside the towns being erased from the official map

Residents describe quiet evictions, missing records, and the roads that disappear before dawn.

At first light, the road is almost empty. A delivery truck passes the last marked intersection, then turns toward a neighborhood that no longer appears on the city’s newest maps.

Residents say the change arrived quietly: an address missing from one form, a utility record unavailable on another. No single document announced that the town had disappeared.
