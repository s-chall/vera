# News page override

The News page intentionally overrides the master dashboard-like direction.

- Purpose: fast scanning and reading, not marketing.
- Layout: one centered feed column; no hero or contextual right rail.
- Navigation: persistent adaptive product navigation remains outside the feed.
- Search: first control in the content region.
- Filters: exactly Journalists and Activists.
- Cards: use separators rather than independent elevated cards.
- Copy: only author, date, verification, post summary, linked headline, and engagement counts.
- Color: use Vera's light paper surface, dark ink, verification blue, and restrained orange for funding.
- Typography: DM Sans for interface and post text; Newsreader only for linked article headlines.
- Motion: no automatic motion; state changes are immediate with a subtle color transition.
