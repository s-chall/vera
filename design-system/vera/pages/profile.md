# Journalist profile

## Intent

The journalist and their reporting lead the page. Bitcoin is a compact account detail, not a trading dashboard.

## Structure

- Profile photo with an explicit upload control, publishing pseudonym, verification state, short beat description, and settings.
- One full-width, low-height Bitcoin row containing the BTC amount, approximate USD value, Withdraw, and Payout history. Balance anchors left and actions anchor right.
- Published and Draft tabs followed by editorial article rows with views, likes, and BTC earned.

## Visual hierarchy

- Newsreader carries the pseudonym, balance, and article titles.
- The portrait and pseudonym are the page’s primary landmark.
- Wallet styling stays neutral, low-height, and intentionally small, with no charts, extra statistics, market colors, or portfolio language.
- Hairline rules group information without turning every region into a card.

## Responsive behavior

- On mobile, the identity header becomes a compact two-column layout and Settings moves below it.
- Wallet controls remain at least 44px tall and wrap without horizontal scrolling.
- Article earnings move beneath the headline while preserving the full title.
