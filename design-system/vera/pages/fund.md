# Total Pool

## Purpose

A public-pool contribution page that combines Open Collective's treasury clarity with GoFundMe's focused donation flow. It must feel like civic infrastructure, not a trading dashboard.

## Content hierarchy

1. Total Pool title (no marketing dek).
2. Current Bitcoin balance and approximate USD value; quiet “View address” link.
3. Contribution amount presets, custom amount, and anonymous toggle.
4. Bitcoin invoice after submission.
5. Recent deposits appear under the balance only when UTXOs exist — no trust/marketing copy.

## Visual rules

- Use Vera's Newsreader and DM Sans pairing, paper background, ink card, and orange action color.
- Keep the pool balance dominant; avoid explanatory marketing copy, portfolio metrics, charts, market prices, and progress targets.
- Every viewport uses a single column, with the balance directly above the contribution form.
- Use Lucide icons only, 44px minimum interaction targets, persistent labels, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
